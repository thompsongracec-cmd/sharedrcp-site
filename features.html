The app

Share. Recap. Discover. Connect. Plan.

Everything your circle loves, kept in one place.

01

Share

Save a restaurant, a recipe or a link in one tap, right from the in-app browser. No copying and pasting, and no screenshots lost in your camera roll. Add your own photos to recipes, so your circle sees how yours actually turned out.

02

Recap

The place for everything you meant to mention and didn't: the funny thing that happened at work, the place you walked past and want to try, the story you forgot to tell at dinner. Add it to your recap, and your circle catches up on the little things that usually slip through.

Recent thoughts

Jot it down the moment it hits you. Recent thoughts holds the stories, ideas and "you have to hear this" moments until your circle's next recap, so nothing gets lost between catch-ups.

03

Discover

Find your next favorite place through people whose taste you trust, not reviews from strangers. Browse what your circle is saving and keep what catches your eye.

04

Connect

Circles are small and invite-only. Stay close to the people who matter, see what they're loving, and share without performing for anyone else.

05

Plan

Turn "we should go there sometime" into an actual date. Pick a restaurant from your saved places or a recipe for a dinner in, choose a time, and your circle can see it and join.

The calendar

A shared calendar for your circle, with every dinner, get-together and night in on one view. Plans link straight back to the restaurant or recipe they came from, so the details are always one tap away. Sync it with your phone's calendar so your plans show up next to everything else, and once a plan happens, it shows up in your weekly recap.

Lists

Shared lists for everything your circle is working toward. To-do lists split up the details before a dinner party or a birthday: who's bringing what, who's booking the table, who's picking up the cake. Everyone checks things off as they go, so no one has to send the "did anyone get the wine?" text.

Trip lists keep a getaway in one place: where you're staying, the restaurants you've saved, reservation times and what to pack. Everyone sees the same plan, and anything you add from a saved place links straight back to it.
